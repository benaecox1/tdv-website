## Who we are

The Director's Vision, LLC ("TDV," "we," "us," or "our") is a business operations company registered in Tennessee. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of working with you.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you submit our inquiry form, book a call, or email us, we may collect:

- Your name, email address, and phone number
- Your business name, website, and social media handles
- Details you share about your business, goals, and the support you're looking for
- How you heard about us

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, ours may collect basic technical data such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and approximate location, through cookies and similar tools.

## How we use it

- To respond to your inquiry and schedule discovery conversations
- To prepare recommendations and proposals for your business
- To deliver and manage the services you engage us for
- To send updates or resources you've opted in to receive
- To understand how our website is used and improve it
- To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

## How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host, scheduling tool, CRM and project management tools, email platform, and payment processor. These providers may only use your information to perform services for us.

As TDV grows, your information may be shared with the TDV operations partner or team member assigned to support you, who is held to the same standards of confidentiality. We may also disclose information when required by law.

## Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand traffic and improve your experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

## Email communications

If you join our email list, we'll send content and updates related to our work. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time.

## Client information

When you become a client, we may have access to information about your business, tools, team, and customers. We treat this information as confidential, use it only to provide our services, and handle it as described in your client agreement.

## Retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, and we will honor requests as required.

## Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
